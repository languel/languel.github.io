Liubo Borissov

Artist and educator · Associate Professor, Digital Arts and Animation, Pratt Institute, Brooklyn

I make interactive, audiovisual and mixed-reality work and the tools to make it, and I teach creative coding, physical computing, mixed realities and creative practice with AI. I trained as a physicist (Ph.D., Columbia) and an interactive-media artist (NYU ITP).

Current research interests include means and measures of creativity.
